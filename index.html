GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Some quick answers before you get in touch.

What is Studio HASH iNf Lk?+

Studio HASH iNf Lk is a digital-first studio bringing together graphics, technology, photography and videography, and music creation under one main brand.

How do I request a project?+

Choose the relevant sub-brand and contact us by email or WhatsApp with a short description of your requirements. An inquiry is not a confirmed booking or order.

Which services are available right now?+

iNfpirex is live for graphics projects. iNfysona is accepting inquiries for small music and audio projects. iNfyzora is coming soon, and iNframex is not yet available.

Can I download software from this website?+

When software is officially released, its product page will show the version, requirements and verified download link. Unreleased products will not have active download buttons.

Do you accept online payments through this website?+

This initial website does not process payments. Pricing, project scope, availability and payment arrangements will be discussed directly before an order is confirmed.
